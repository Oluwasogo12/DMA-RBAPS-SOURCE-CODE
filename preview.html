Physics · Medium
UTME 2023 Medium Mechanics

A body of mass 5 kg moving with velocity 20 m/s collides with a stationary body of mass 15 kg. If they stick together after the collision, what is their common velocity?

  • A2 m/s
  • B5 m/s
  • C10 m/s — correct by momentum conservation
  • D20 m/s
Incorrect — the answer is C

By conservation of momentum: m₁v₁ = (m₁ + m₂)v₂ → 5 × 20 = 20 × v₂ → v₂ = 5 m/s. The correct answer depends on the total mass. Momentum is always conserved in collisions.
